Q4 · What did you learn? Technically, and about negotiation and marketplaces

What we learned

Technical draft
  • Code decides, the LLM writes. On a 15 s tick, latency is a rule: Haiku ≈ 2 s vs 9.2 s when the LLM decided.
  • The generator can't grade itself. A separate verifier caught errors the author missed, in this very deck too.
Negotiation draft
  • Decay is per exchange, so silence is free. Hold instead of talking.
  • Take the good offer when it's there. 23 Duels III deals closed below an offer the rival had already made (≈ 197 P, the monitor's price check).
Marketplace draft
  • Value created, not activity. A trade with a rival lifts the rival as much as you: CHA-11, +1.48 each.
  • Pushing partners moved trades; the bounty didn't. 0 fills at 10:45, 8 at the close; the bounty paid nobody.
  • Nobody beat the free stall. 0.5 in all eleven Market Tests: no bond needed.
