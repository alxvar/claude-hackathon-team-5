Q3 · Why that way?
Three decisions we'd defend.
1
Code decides, the LLM writes.

Price, day and accept are code; Haiku writes the words in ≈ 2 s vs 9.4 s when the LLM decided. Guards in code.

Because a 15 s tick doesn't wait.
2
2 writers · 1 orchestrator · 14 watchers.

Two roles act, one Chief decides, fourteen read-only daemons sense; every hard limit is a written GUARDRAIL line.

3
Redesigned every night, pressure-tested.

Saturday night the duelist went code-first (547 tests green) and was checked on the simulator; Sunday 07:25 contrarian reviews of CHA, duels and market fixed the plan; verifier agents re-checked every claim; 163 tests guard the bots.

Q4 · What did you learn?

What we learned.

TechnicalLucas · Aleks
Real-time shared context is everything.
Day one, three people and their agents worked in silos; by Sunday every agent saw every update as it happened.
NegotiationAleks · Lucas
Time can beat price.
A fast good deal beats a slow perfect one; and people still decide: alliances made in the room outweighed agent tactics.
MarketplaceDani · Aleks
Trust moves trades, not fees.
Zero commission wasn't enough; trust and returned favours brought trades to our market. Incentives can break.
Three humans set the limits. Claude ran the bazaar, and checked itself.
