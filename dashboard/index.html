Every leaderboard interval where our own part (after field drift) moved ≥ 1.5 points or we changed two places, newest first. What: our trades, a page completed, or other teams trading on our venue (that value scores market points for us). Why: the team decisions in intel/directives.md that name the same card or venue.

    Why our score moved

    One bar per leaderboard refresh (about every 5-10 ticks). Blue: our negotiating (our trades, pages, dealers). Green: our market (Market Tests and trades between other teams on our venue).                 What the words mean

                Venue
                Where the trade happened: El Rastro (the house market) or a stall a team opened. ★ = ours (v10).
                Sold for cash / card for card
                One team gives cards and the other pays primas (P), or both give cards (a swap).
                Fee
                What the venue charged the team that accepted. El Rastro: 5 % + 1 P per card; most team stalls: 0.
                × book
                Price compared with the card's reference price for its rarity: 0.7× = 30 % below, 2× = double.
                Collects / gets rid of
                From each team's behaviour (Rivals tab): a team that buys or bids for a set collects it; one that mostly sells it gets rid of it.
                Value created
                How much more the buyer values the cards than the seller (book × the gap between their set multipliers). Ours is exact; the others are the Analyst's estimates in intel/multipliers.json [L], so "—" when unknown. On our venue this is what scores our market part: a minus there costs us.
                Top 4
                One of the teams is in the current top 4: the ones we never feed.
